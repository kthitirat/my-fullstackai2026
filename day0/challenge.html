Title: A Simple Morning in My Life








Every morning, my alarm clock rings at 6:30 AM. At first, I feel a little sleepy, so I stretch my arms and take a deep breath before getting out of bed.








After waking up, I walk to the bathroom to wash my face and brush my teeth. Then, I spend ten minutes doing some light stretching or yoga to wake my body up. Next, I go to the kitchen to prepare my breakfast. I usually make a cup of hot coffee, some toast, and eat a fresh banana.









While eating, I check my phone for important messages and read the news headlines for the day. Around 7:45 AM, I get dressed, grab my bag, and step out of the house to start my commute to work or school. Even though it is a simple routine, starting the morning calmly always gives me a good energy for the rest of the day.
