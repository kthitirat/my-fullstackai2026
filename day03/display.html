LOROM

In July 2014, LOROM officially opened its third wire harness and cable assembly plant in China. The investment increases LOROM’s cable assembly capacity in China and underscores LOROM’s aggressive expansion in China and the Asia Pacific region. This highly automated, assembly plant will be flexible enough to produce different types of cable harnesses and cable assemblies.
